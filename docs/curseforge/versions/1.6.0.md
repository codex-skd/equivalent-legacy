<h2>v1.6.0 - Own Controls Category</h2>

<h3>Feature</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Keybindings now have their own Controls category</strong>: Equivalent Legacy's 6 keybindings (Helmet Effects, Boots Effects, Charge, Extra Function, Fire Projectile, Change Mode) were registered under vanilla Minecraft's "Gameplay" category, mixing them with base game keybinds. They now live in their own "Equivalent Legacy" category in the Controls menu, making them easy to find and rebind.</li>
</ul>
