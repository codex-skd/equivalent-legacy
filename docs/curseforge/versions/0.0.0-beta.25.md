<h2>v0.0.0-beta.25 - Full Star Recipes</h2>

<p>The endgame crafting alternatives using a maxed Klein Star Omega.</p>

<h3>Added</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Full Klein Star ingredient</strong>: a custom recipe ingredient matching a Klein Star Omega charged to its full 16M capacity.</li>
<li><strong>Full Star Gem Armor</strong>: alternative recipes for the Gem Helmet/Chestplate/Leggings/Boots, crafted with the Red Matter pieces + arcana items + a <em>maxed</em> Klein Star Omega.</li>
<li><strong>Full Star Tome</strong>: alternative recipes for the Tome of Knowledge using a maxed Klein Star Omega.</li>
</ul>

<h3>Notes</h3>
<blockquote>These are alternative crafting routes (the original's endgame option); the regular recipes remain. This completes the optional endgame variants of the port.</blockquote>
