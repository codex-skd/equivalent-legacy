<h2>v1.0.0 - Equivalent Legacy</h2>

<p>The first stable release. Equivalent Legacy is a full port of the classic ProjectE / Equivalent Exchange 2 experience for Minecraft 26.2 / NeoForge, forked from <a href="https://github.com/Yaskulsky/projecte-26-port">Equivox</a> by Yaskulsky (MIT) and the original ProjectE codebase (SinKillerJ and contributors).</p>

<h3>Core EMC System</h3>
<ul>
<li>Full EMC value mapping, item knowledge and transmutation: learn items and convert EMC between them.</li>
<li><strong>Philosopher's Stone</strong>, <strong>Transmutation Table</strong> and <strong>Transmutation Tablet</strong> GUIs.</li>
<li><strong>Tome of Knowledge</strong> unlocks everything; <strong>Covalence Dusts</strong> (Low/Medium/High) power the toolchain.</li>
</ul>

<h3>Items &amp; Toolchain</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Alchemical fuels</strong>: Alchemical Coal, Mobius Fuel, Aeternalis Fuel and their storage blocks.</li>
<li><strong>Dark Matter and Red Matter</strong>, their blocks, and the full <strong>tool and armor toolchains</strong> (sword, pickaxe, axe, shovel, hoe, shears, hammer, katar, morning star) with a <strong>charge/AOE system</strong> (shift-right-click to charge, area mining and area attacks).</li>
<li><strong>Gem Armor</strong> and <strong>Full Star</strong> endgame recipes (maxed Klein Star Omega).</li>
<li><strong>Klein Stars</strong> Ein through Omega: portable EMC batteries (equippable via Curios).</li>
<li><strong>Arcana set</strong>: rings, bands, amulets, stones, talisman and watch with passive effects, all equippable in Curios slots.</li>
<li>Nova catalysts, Divining Rods, Destruction Catalyst, Hyperkinetic/Catalytic Lenses, Mercurial Eye, Interdiction Torch, and more.</li>
</ul>

<h3>Machines &amp; Storage</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Collectors, Relays and Condensers</strong> with GUIs, sharing a <strong>persistent per-dimension EMC network</strong>.</li>
<li><strong>Dark and Red Matter Furnaces</strong> (4x and 8x smelting).</li>
<li><strong>Alchemical Chest</strong> and <strong>16 colored Alchemical Bags</strong>.</li>
</ul>

<h3>Requirements</h3>
<ul>
<li>Minecraft 26.2 / NeoForge 26.2.0.37-beta</li>
<li><a href="https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/curios">Curios API</a> 15.0.0+ (project <code>1579340</code>)</li>
</ul>
