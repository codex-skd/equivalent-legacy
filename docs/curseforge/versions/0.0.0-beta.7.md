<h2>v0.0.0-beta.7 - Klein Star Line and Crafting</h2>

<p>The Klein Star grows into a full battery line, and everything finally has recipes and a command.</p>

<h3>Added</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Klein Star Zwei, Drei, Vier, Sphere, Omega</strong>: the complete portable EMC battery line. Each tier stores double the EMC (1M to 16M) and is crafted from 4 of the previous tier (shapeless). Same charge/discharge behavior as Ein.</li>
<li><strong>Crafting recipes</strong>: Philosopher's Stone, Dark Matter, Klein Star Ein and the higher tiers are now craftable in survival (ingredients adapted to vanilla until the alchemical fuels are implemented).</li>
<li><strong>Commands</strong>: <code>/equivalent_legacy emc</code> shows your EMC; <code>/equivalent_legacy emc give &lt;player&gt; &lt;amount&gt;</code> grants EMC (operators only).</li>
</ul>

<h3>Notes</h3>
<blockquote>Still early development. No collectors/condensers/relays, Red Matter, tools/armor, alchemical bags/chest, or Curios support yet.</blockquote>
