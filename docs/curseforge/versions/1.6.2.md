<h2>v1.6.2 - Item Effect Tooltips</h2>

<h3>Fix</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>17 items with active/passive effects didn't explain that effect in their tooltip</strong>: several rings, stones, and Gem armor pieces alter the world or the player while equipped/active (repel nearby mobs, automatically feed/heal you, freeze the nearby area, ignite/extinguish fire, pull dropped items, drain your XP, negate fall damage, speed up nearby blocks, etc.), but the tooltip either said nothing about it, or only showed the active mode's name (e.g. "Ignition") without explaining what it actually does. Added a tooltip line describing the real effect to: <strong>Swiftwolf's Rending Gale</strong>, <strong>Arcana Ring</strong>, <strong>Archangel Smite</strong>, <strong>Black Hole Band</strong>, <strong>Body Stone</strong>, <strong>Harvest Goddess Band</strong>, <strong>Ignition Ring</strong>, <strong>Life Stone</strong>, <strong>Soul Stone</strong>, <strong>Mind Stone</strong>, <strong>Watch of Flowing Time</strong>, <strong>Void Ring</strong>, <strong>Zero Ring</strong>, and the <strong>Gem Chestplate/Helmet/Leggings/Boots</strong>.</li>
</ul>

<h3>Notes</h3>
<ul>
<li>Fully translated into all 11 supported languages plus English (source).</li>
</ul>
