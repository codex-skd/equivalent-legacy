<h2>v0.0.0-beta.16 - Arcana Curio Items</h2>

<p>The classic rings, bands, amulets and stones arrive, with passive effects while carried.</p>

<h3>Added</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Rings &amp; Bands</strong>: Iron Band, Black Hole Band (item magnet), Gem of Eternal Density, Harvest Goddess Band, Ignition Ring (right-click ignites), Swiftwolf's Rending Gale (speed + jump), Void Ring (absorbs EMC from nearby items), Zero Ring (right-click extinguishes fire), Arcana Ring.</li>
<li><strong>Amulets &amp; Stones</strong>: Evertide Amulet (water breathing, right-click places water), Volcanite Amulet (fire resistance, right-click places lava), Life Stone (regeneration), Soul Stone (regeneration + slow falling), Body Stone (saturation), Mind Stone (speed + jump).</li>
<li><strong>Talisman &amp; Watch</strong>: Repair Talisman (slowly repairs your items), Watch of Flowing Time (haste).</li>
<li>Effects apply while the item is in your inventory; all are equippable in Curios slots when Curios is installed (belt/necklace/ring tags).</li>
</ul>

<h3>Notes</h3>
<blockquote>Effects work from the inventory; Curios-equipped items are displayed but passive effects currently require the item to be in your inventory. Still missing: transmutation tablet/table, nova items, furnaces, divining rods and the destruction catalyst.</blockquote>
