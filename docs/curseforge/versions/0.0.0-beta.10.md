<h2>v0.0.0-beta.10 - Dark and Red Matter Toolchain</h2>

<p>The full combat and labor toolchain for both matters, including 3x3 area mining.</p>

<h3>Added</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Dark Matter tools</strong>: Sword, Pickaxe, Axe, Shovel, Hoe, Shears and Hammer.</li>
<li><strong>Red Matter tools</strong>: Sword, Pickaxe, Axe, Shovel, Hoe, Shears, Hammer, Katar and Morning Star.</li>
<li><strong>Hammer</strong>: mines a 3x3 area aligned to the face you break, with proper item drops and durability cost per block.</li>
<li><strong>Armor</strong>: full Helmet/Chestplate/Leggings/Boots sets for both matters, rendered through new equipment assets (visible on the player model), with netherite-tier protection plus toughness/knockback on Red Matter.</li>
<li>Tools are netherite-tier harvesting, very high durability, and Red Matter upgrades craft from the Dark Matter versions (original ProjectE progression).</li>
</ul>

<h3>Notes</h3>
<blockquote>Tools and armor are simplified versus ProjectE for now: no charge/AOE attack system on swords, no EMC durability. Still no collectors/condensers/relays, alchemical bags/chest, or Curios support.</blockquote>
