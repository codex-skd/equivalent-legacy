<h2>v0.0.0-beta.12 - Alchemical Bag and Alchemical Chest</h2>

<p>Portable and stationary EMC-free storage: the 13-slot Bag and Chest.</p>

<h3>Added</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Alchemical Bag</strong>: a portable 13-slot inventory stored directly on the item. Right-click to open; contents persist on the item and survive in your inventory.</li>
<li><strong>Alchemical Chest</strong>: a 13-slot block that opens with a right-click and drops its contents when broken.</li>
</ul>

<h3>Notes</h3>
<blockquote>Single bag color for now (colored variants and Curios support may come later). Recipes adapted from the original: covalence dusts are replaced by alchemical fuels until those items exist.</blockquote>
